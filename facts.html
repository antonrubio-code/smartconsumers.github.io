Cool Facts About Shopping & Consuming 🛒

📦 Online shopping is growing fast.

More and more people prefer buying online because it’s easy and often cheaper than stores.

🛍️ Impulse buying is common.

Shiny displays and “limited offers” make many people buy things they don’t really need.

💳 Credit cards can trick you.

It’s easy to overspend when you don’t see the cash leaving your hands right away.

🧾 Always check expiration dates.

Products on sale might be close to expiry — smart shoppers always double-check labels.

♻️ Packaging makes a big difference.

Eco-friendly packaging reduces waste and is better for the environment.

🧴 Everyday products have active ingredients.

Soaps, cosmetics, and cleaners often contain chemicals that can help — or harm if misused.

⚠️ Ads influence your choices.

Colorful marketing and slogans are designed to make you buy, even if you don’t need it.

💡 Reading reviews helps.

Checking what other buyers say online can save you from wasting money on bad products.

🛒 Shopping lists save money.

People who make a list before shopping spend less and avoid unnecessary purchases.

🌍 Your purchase has impact.

Buying local products supports small businesses and reduces carbon footprint from shipping.
