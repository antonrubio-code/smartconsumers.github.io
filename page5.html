Things to Remember When Buying

What Makes a Product Truly Worth It?

“It’s easy to get tricked by colorful packaging or low prices, but smart shoppers look deeper. Safety, quality, and usefulness matter more than looks. Buying wisely protects your health, your wallet, and the environment.”

Always check expiration dates. → Expired items are unsafe and may no longer work properly.
Look for safety seals and real labels. → Fake or broken seals may mean the product is unsafe.
Choose eco-friendly products. → Green products are safer for you and the environment.
Don’t be fooled by packaging—check ingredients. → Sometimes shiny packaging hides harmful contents.
Buy only what you need. → Avoid waste, save money, and use products while they’re still safe.
