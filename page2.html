Don’t Trash It Wrong!

Disposal of Consumer Products

Where Does Your Waste Really Go?

“Throwing something away doesn’t mean it disappears. Some waste pollutes water, some damages soil, and some can even be reused. Knowing how to dispose of products properly helps protect people, animals, and the planet.”

Separate biodegradable, non-biodegradable, and recyclable items. → Sorting makes it easier to recycle and keep the environment clean.
Never toss chemicals, batteries, or medicines in regular trash. → These can poison soil, water, or even people if not disposed of properly.
Reuse jars, bottles, and containers when possible. → Old containers can be used again, reducing waste and saving money.
Remember: Reduce ♻️ Reuse ♻️ Recycle. → This 3R rule helps lessen trash and protect our planet.
