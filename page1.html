Proper Usage of Consumer Products

Why Does Proper Use Matter?

“When we buy products, we often think they’ll work perfectly no matter what. But using them the wrong way can waste money, cause accidents, or even harm us. Using items the right way makes life easier, safer, and smarter.”

Always follow instructions on labels. → Labels tell you how to use the product properly. Skipping them can lead to accidents.
Don’t overuse—more doesn’t mean better. → Using too much soap, medicine, or makeup can waste money and even harm your body.
Keep away from kids if dangerous. → Children might play with cleaning agents or sharp items, which can be risky.
Use products only for their real purpose. → Shampoo is for hair, not for cleaning the floor. Wrong use can cause damage or danger.
