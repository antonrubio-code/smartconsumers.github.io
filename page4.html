Health-Related Risks

What’s the Risk Behind Everyday Items?

“Not all products are as safe as they look. Cosmetics, cleaning agents, or even food additives can harm us if we’re careless. Knowing the risks helps us avoid sickness and keep our bodies healthy.”

Too much cosmetics may cause skin problems. → Heavy use of makeup or lotions can lead to rashes or allergies.
Inhaling cleaning agents can hurt your lungs. → Breathing in strong chemicals can cause coughing or even long-term damage.
Expired food additives = stomach pain. → Eating spoiled food can cause food poisoning or tummy aches.
Unsafe chemicals = serious illness. → Some products have toxins that may cause lasting health problems if misused.
