“Better Safe Than Sorry”

Precautionary Measures

Why Should We Be Careful?

“Some products look harmless but can be dangerous when used the wrong way. Labels, safety caps, and warnings exist for a reason. Taking small precautions can prevent accidents and keep you and your family safe.”

Wear gloves or masks when needed. → Some cleaning products or sprays can hurt your skin or lungs without protection.
Store flammable items away from heat. → Things like alcohol and aerosol cans can catch fire if left near stoves or sunlight.
Don’t mix unknown chemicals. → Mixing bleach and other cleaners can create poisonous gas.
Keep medicine in the right place, out of reach of children. → Proper storage prevents kids from accidentally swallowing them.
